# Mohan Meakin Report 2023-2024

This report provides a clear exploration of Mohan Meakin Limited's operational landscape for 2023-2024, focusing on aspects that extend well beyond mere financial performance. It delves into the robust framework of corporate governance, detailed board dynamics, and the systematic approaches to internal controls and regulatory compliance that shape the company's identity. By examining initiatives in corporate social responsibility, employee welfare, and ethical oversight, the report highlights the non-financial dimensions that are essential to understanding Mohan Meakin's overall performance and industry standing.

The analysis unfolds with an emphasis on measurable key performance indicators across areas such as governance, human resource management, and community impact. It underlines the company's commitment to structured internal review mechanisms, diverse and inclusive board composition, and proactive social initiatives, all of which contribute to a sustained operational excellence. This narrative not only reflects the current practices but also sets the stage for future progress by presenting a detailed account of the practices and policies that sustain Mohan Meakin's market reputation and stakeholder trust.

## Internal Control Systems and Vigil Mechanism

**Mohan Meakin's internal control systems are both comprehensive and effective, ensuring robust asset protection and adherence to ethical practices.**

The company's approach is structured around three key pillars:

- **Internal Control Systems**
  - *Robust Procedures:* The company maintains a strong, independent, and adequate system of internal control procedures tailored to its size and business nature.
  - *Comprehensive Audits:* An extensive program of internal audits, including reviews by management and leading Chartered Accountant firms, is conducted across all branches and operations.
  - *Asset Protection & Reporting Integrity:* Controls ensure that all **assets** are safeguarded against unauthorized use, and that **transactions** are authorized, accurately recorded, and correctly reported.
  - *Management Oversight:* Findings and recommendations from internal audits are routinely reviewed by top management and the Audit Committee, with oversight confirmed by the **CEO and CFO Certificate**.
  - *Financial Controls:* The internal financial controls for preparing statements show no material weaknesses in either design or operation.

- **Vigil Mechanism**
  - *Whistleblower Policy:* In compliance with sections **177(9) & (10)** of the Companies Act, 2013, a vigilant mechanism enables directors and employees to report concerns related to unethical conduct, fraud, or code of conduct violations.
  - *Corporate Transparency:* This mechanism is part of the company's broader policy on corporate transparency and is integral to the Corporate Governance Report.

- **Board Policy & Corporate Governance**
  - *Board-Endorsed Policies:* The board has approved a range of policies that ensure compliance with regulations and support the company's commitment to ethical practices.
  - *Committees for Oversight:* All requisite board committees are in place, most having a majority of **independent directors**, to monitor and strengthen governance standards.
  - *Code of Conduct Compliance:* The Board of Directors and senior management affirm their commitment to adhering to the company's **code of conduct**, reinforcing internal ethical behavior and compliance.

This structured framework of internal controls, vigilant mechanisms, and board policies reinforces Mohan Meakin's commitment to maintaining high standards of **corporate governance** and operational integrity while aligning with best practices in the industry.

## Regulatory Compliance and Secretarial Standards

**Mohan Meakin ensures rigorous compliance with environmental, corporate governance, and insider trading regulations to uphold operational integrity and market trust.**

### Key Areas of Compliance

- **Environmental Compliance**
  - *Standards and Guidelines*: Adheres to environmental and safety standards as mandated by the **Pollution Control Board** and other governmental authorities.
  - *Sustainability*: Compliance with these norms not only ensures operational legality but also aligns with broader **sustainability trends**, enhancing the company's reputation.

- **Corporate Governance and Secretarial Standards**
  - *Board and Governance*: 
    - Adheres to **Corporate Governance norms** in the constitution of the **Board of Directors**.
    - Maintains all requisite board committees designed to strengthen governance in line with internationally accepted practices.
  - *Secretarial Best Practices*:
    - Complies with applicable **Secretarial Standards SS-1** (Meetings of the Board of Directors) and **SS-2** (General Meetings).
    - A **Compliance Certificate of Practicing Company Secretary** is annexed in the Annual Report, confirming adherence to conditions of Corporate Governance as stipulated under Schedule V(E) of the **SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015**.
    - Implements a **Familiarization Program for Independent Directors** to provide in-depth insights into the company's business.
  - *Workplace Ethics*:
    - Enforces a zero-tolerance policy towards sexual harassment, in line with the **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention and Redressal) Act, 2013**.

- **Insider Trading Policies**
  - *Code of Conduct*: 
    - Operates a comprehensive **Code of Conduct for the Prevention of Insider Trading** applicable to designated employees, directors, officers, and senior managers.
    - Outlines procedures and disclosure requirements for personal securities transactions, ensuring that fiduciary duties to shareholders are maintained.
  - *Price Sensitive Information*:
    - The code ensures timely disclosure of **Price Sensitive Information** to the investor community.
    - Utilizes a dedicated **Structural Digital Database (SDD)** for capturing unpublished price-sensitive information.
  - *Regulatory Certification*:
    - Files quarterly certificates with the Stock Exchanges, signed by **M/s AKP & Associates**, affirming compliance with the **SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015**.
